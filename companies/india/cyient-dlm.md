## Investment thesis

Cyient DLM is an aerospace/defence-focused electronics manufacturing platform with a growing box-build and high-value manufacturing opportunity. The thesis rests on customer qualification, long program lives, increasing outsourcing and a strong order pipeline.

## Current evidence

Q1 FY27 revenue grew about 34% YoY, EBITDA about 56%, and EBITDA margin reached roughly 10.5%. The order book reached a record ~₹2,600 crore, with management pointing to a 1.5x book-to-bill and strong FY27 visibility. ### Why these scores

**Business Potential 8.0:** Attractive aerospace/defence EMS TAM, long qualification cycles and potential to expand into higher-value box-build programs.

**Probability 6.5:** Order-book visibility and book-to-bill are encouraging, but the business still needs to prove sustained ROIC improvement, cash conversion and margin durability at scale.

**Investment Attractiveness 4.0:** At roughly ₹6,700 crore market cap, 100X means ~₹6.7 lakh crore. A 40x terminal P/E would require ~₹16,700 crore PAT and, at 20% net margin, ~₹83,500 crore revenue. The current valuation already assumes strong execution.

## Key risks

- Low current ROIC/ROCE
- Customer/program concentration
- Order-book conversion and execution
- Working-capital requirements
- Valuation / multiple compression
- Dependence on aerospace/defence program ramps

## Verdict

**CORE — ACCUMULATE**, but with a strong requirement for earnings and return-on-capital confirmation. The business opportunity is attractive; valuation currently prevents a high Investment Attractiveness score.
