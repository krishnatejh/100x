## Investment thesis

Azad is a high-value precision-engineering supplier with exposure to aerospace & defence, power generation and other complex rotating/stationary components. The strongest part of the thesis is the combination of qualification barriers, dedicated OEM capacity and a very large order book relative to the current revenue base. Management reported an order book of roughly ₹6,500 crore at FY26 exit and expects a 25%+ multi-year top-line trajectory as newly commissioned facilities ramp.

## Current evidence

Q1 FY27 consolidated revenue was about ₹172.6 crore, up 25.9% YoY, with EBITDA margin around 37% and PAT about ₹35.7 crore. FY26 aerospace & defence revenue reached roughly ₹101 crore, while the order book remained around ₹6,500 crore. The company is investing heavily in dedicated facilities for global OEM programs.

## 100X Score

- **Business Potential:** 8.8 / 10

- **Investment Attractiveness:** 4.0 / 10
- **Overall 100X:** 7.0 / 10

### Why these scores

**Business Potential 8.8:** Large global OEM opportunity, qualification moat, dedicated factories, very high order-book visibility and potential movement from components into assemblies/subassemblies.

**Probability 7.0:** Customer validation and order visibility are strong, but execution across a rapidly expanding asset base, customer concentration and return-on-capital recovery remain important uncertainties.

**Investment Attractiveness 4.0:** The business can become much larger, but the market already prices in a very large amount of future growth. A 100X from a market cap around ₹17,900 crore requires roughly ₹17.9 lakh crore; valuation leaves little margin for execution misses.

## 100X mathematics

At approximately ₹17,900 crore current market cap, 100X implies about ₹17.9 lakh crore. At a 40x terminal P/E this would require roughly ₹44,700 crore PAT; at a 20% net margin that implies about ₹2.24 lakh crore revenue. This is mathematically possible over 25+ years but requires sustained ~20% revenue compounding plus durable margins and high returns on incremental capital.

## Key risks

- Customer/program concentration
- Execution and ramp-up risk across new facilities
- ROIC/ROCE remaining below the cost of capital
- Very high valuation / multiple compression
- Working-capital intensity and dilution

## Verdict

**CORE — ACCUMULATE SLOWLY**, but the current valuation is the dominant constraint. The score should be revisited after evidence that the new capacity converts into revenue and higher incremental returns.
