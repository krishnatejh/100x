## Investment thesis

PG Electroplast combines EMS with consumer-durables manufacturing and has built significant scale in air-conditioners, refrigerators, washing machines, plastics and related products. The long-term opportunity is localisation plus outsourcing by consumer brands, with substantial room for capacity expansion.

## Current evidence

Q1 FY27 revenue was ~₹2,034 crore, up 35.2% YoY, while PAT rose 13.8% to ~₹76 crore. EBITDA margin was ~7.7%, showing that revenue growth is currently running ahead of profit growth. ### Why these scores

**Business Potential 8.5:** Large and expanding Indian consumer-durables manufacturing opportunity, multiple product categories and scope to become a major outsourced manufacturing partner.

**Probability 7.0:** Strong growth history and scale-up capability support the thesis, but the current low ROCE/ROIC and execution intensity mean the company still needs to prove that growth translates into superior incremental returns.

**Investment Attractiveness 5.0:** At ~₹16,000 crore market cap, 100X implies ~₹16 lakh crore. At 40x terminal P/E this requires ~₹40,000 crore PAT; at 20% net margin, ~₹2 lakh crore revenue. This is feasible only with decades of sustained growth and significant margin improvement, while the present valuation is already elevated.

## Key risks

- Low current capital efficiency
- Commodity/margin pressure
- Heavy capex and execution requirements
- Customer concentration / bargaining power
- Valuation risk

## Verdict

**CORE — ACCUMULATE**, but only while the thesis continues to show improving margins and incremental returns. Revenue growth alone is not enough for the 100X case.
