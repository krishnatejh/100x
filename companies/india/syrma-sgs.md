## Investment thesis

Syrma SGS is a diversified electronics manufacturing and design platform with exposure to automotive, consumer, industrial, healthcare, IT/railways and exports. Its potential comes from increasing electronics outsourcing, product mix improvement, higher-value ODM/industrial work and new ecosystem investments.

## Current evidence

Q1 FY27 revenue was ~₹1,604 crore, up 67% YoY; EBITDA was ~₹177 crore and PAT ~₹106 crore, up 72% and 112% respectively. The company also added a Kaga Electronics JV, acquired Elcome Integrated Systems and is progressing its PCB project. FY26 ROCE was ~14.9% and current ROIC is around 15.3%, materially better than several other EMS candidates. citeturn2search0turn3search1

The key issue is valuation: market cap is roughly ₹28,000 crore and trailing P/E is around 75x, despite the company's still-maturing return profile. citeturn1search4turn3search1

### Why these scores

**Business Potential 8.5:** Large electronics outsourcing TAM, diversified end markets, export opportunity and potential to move further into higher-value manufacturing and design-linked programs.

**Probability 7.0:** Strong current growth, improving ROIC and diversified verticals support the thesis. Risks remain around customer concentration, working capital and the ability to sustain margins while scaling.

**Investment Attractiveness 4.0:** At ~₹28,000 crore market cap, 100X implies ~₹28 lakh crore. At 40x terminal P/E this requires ~₹70,000 crore PAT; at 20% net margin, ~₹3.5 lakh crore revenue. The starting valuation makes the hurdle very high.

## Key risks

- Valuation / multiple compression
- Working-capital intensity
- Customer concentration
- Margin pressure as scale expands
- Capex and integration execution
- Dilution from future growth funding

## Verdict

**CORE — ACCUMULATE SLOWLY.** The business quality and growth runway are attractive, but current valuation requires sustained high growth and improving returns for a satisfactory long-term outcome.
