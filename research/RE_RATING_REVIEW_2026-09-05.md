## 1. E2E Networks

**Current:** WATCH  
**Proposed:** Re-rate upward; potential CORE candidate pending full work  
**Priority:** #1

### Evidence
- Q1 FY27 revenue was approximately ₹156.8 Cr, up ~334% YoY and ~64% QoQ.
- Q1 FY27 XBRL EBITDA was approximately ₹129.3 Cr and PAT approximately ₹43.9 Cr.
- Growth was supported by the B200 GPU cluster going live and improving GPU utilisation.
- E2E announced a binding term sheet of approximately ₹1,000 Cr with an Indian sovereign AI organisation for NVIDIA Blackwell GPU cloud infrastructure and associated services.

### 100X interpretation
This is the most material change in the WATCH universe because the company may be moving from a conventional cloud-hosting story toward a scalable AI infrastructure platform. The opportunity is potentially enormous, but the current quarter contains unusual GPU economics and the reported margin profile needs reconciliation before extrapolation.

### What must be proven
- Sustainable GPU utilisation and unit economics.
- Capital intensity and financing requirements as GPU capacity scales.
- Customer concentration and durability of the ₹1,000 Cr term sheet.
- Cash conversion after depreciation/capex.
- Whether the economics can scale without destroying ROIC.
- Current market cap and valuation versus a 10X/50X/100X outcome.

**Decision:** Research immediately; do not upgrade solely because of the AI narrative or term sheet.

## 2. Kaynes Technology

**Current:** WATCH  
**Proposed:** Re-rate upward; potential CORE candidate pending full work  
**Priority:** #2

### Evidence
- Kaynes is advancing its semiconductor/OSAT and PCB manufacturing expansion.
- Current reporting indicates commercial production from key semiconductor/PCB facilities is targeted around Q3 FY27.
- The semiconductor strategy potentially adds a much larger TAM and multiple growth engines beyond the existing EMS business.

### 100X interpretation
Kaynes deserves a fresh 100X review because the business may evolve from an EMS compounder into a broader electronics/semiconductor manufacturing platform. The key question is whether the incremental capital can earn attractive returns and whether the valuation already capitalises a large portion of this future.

### What must be proven
- OSAT economics, customer commitments and utilisation ramp.
- PCB economics and competitive differentiation.
- Incremental ROCE/ROIC after semiconductor capex.
- Balance-sheet/capex requirements and dilution risk.
- 100X market-cap mathematics from the current base.
- Valuation versus Syrma and other EMS peers without relying on relative multiples alone.

**Decision:** Move to top-tier WATCH research queue; no automatic CORE upgrade.

**Proposed:** Re-rate upward  
**Priority:** #3

- EBITDA grew ~36% YoY; PAT grew ~44.9% YoY to ~₹33 Cr.
