# 2026-09-06 — Review of “Companies with good future guidance” list

## Purpose

Screen the supplied list against the 100X framework. Management guidance is treated as evidence, not as a thesis by itself. The key question is whether the company combines a very large runway, scalable economics, credible execution, and a valuation that still permits exceptional long-term returns.

## High-level conclusion

The list is useful as an idea-generation source but is heavily mixed. It contains:

- genuine 100X research candidates;
- good businesses whose current market caps/valuations make 100X mathematically difficult;
- cyclical or capex-driven companies with strong near-term guidance but weaker 100X economics;
- large caps where 50X/100X is effectively ruled out by starting size.

No CORE status changes are made from this screen alone.

## Highest-priority research queue

### 1. E2E Networks — very high priority / FRONTIER candidate

Q1 FY27 revenue was ~₹157 Cr, up ~334% YoY, with ₹44 Cr consolidated profit. The company has also announced a reported ₹1,000 Cr binding term sheet for NVIDIA Blackwell GPU infrastructure/services. This materially strengthens the AI-cloud runway thesis, but the economics are still early and the valuation is extreme: current market cap is roughly ₹12,000 Cr and trailing P/E is ~390x. This belongs in a full 100X mathematics exercise, not a direct upgrade.

**Why interesting:** potentially enormous AI-compute TAM, domestic sovereign-cloud angle, operating leverage.
**Why dangerous:** GPU capex intensity, customer concentration, technology obsolescence, financing/dilution risk, and valuation already embedding extraordinary success.
**Decision:** prioritize full research; do not promote to CORE yet.

### 2. Shivalik Bimetal — high-priority WATCH research

Current market cap is roughly ₹6,500 Cr, P/E ~61x, ROCE ~27%, with a differentiated position in precision bimetals, shunts, contacts and related components. It has a more credible 100X starting-point than the large-cap names in this list, although 100X still requires a very large expansion in revenue and profit.

**Decision:** high-priority comparative research versus Pitti / TD Power / other precision-electrical candidates.

### 3. Pitti Engineering — high-priority WATCH research

Current market cap is roughly ₹4,100 Cr and consolidated P/E around 33x. FY27 quarterly revenue has continued to grow, while the company participates in electrical/precision-engineered components. Valuation is materially more forgiving than many names in this list.

**Decision:** high-priority research; this is one of the better mathematical setups in the list.

### 4. Yash Highvoltage — high-priority small-cap research, but high valuation / liquidity risk

Current market cap is roughly ₹2,800–2,900 Cr, P/E ~73x, ROCE ~29%, ROE ~24%. The transformer-bushing niche has attractive qualification/engineering barriers and the starting market cap makes the 100X mathematics much more feasible than for larger names.

**Decision:** research, but treat SME/liquidity/governance/valuation risk as first-class risks.

### 5. Sudeep Pharma — high-priority pharma specialty-ingredients research

FY26 revenue was ₹642 Cr, EBITDA ₹222 Cr and PAT ₹174 Cr; reported ROCE was ~22.5%. The company has specialty excipient/mineral-active capabilities and a global customer base. However, current market cap is already ~₹12,500–14,000 Cr and valuation is elevated (~70x+ depending on data date), so success does not automatically translate into 100X shareholder returns.

**Decision:** WATCH / research deeply; good business candidate, questionable 100X valuation starting point.

### 6. TD Power Systems — high-quality business, weak 100X mathematics at current size

Current market cap is ~₹23,000 Cr, P/E ~84x and ROCE ~34%. FY27 quarterly numbers show strong growth and high operating margins. This may be an excellent long-term compounder, but the starting size makes a 100X outcome very demanding.

**Decision:** research as a high-quality compounder, not a presumptive 100X candidate.

### 7. KSH International — interesting emerging power/electrical candidate

May 2026 investor material showed a market cap of ~₹48.2 Bn (~₹4,800 Cr), 74.6% promoter ownership and a broad institutional shareholder base. Its starting size is compatible with a 100X screen, but a full operating/ROIC/debt/order-book analysis is still required.

**Decision:** retain high on WATCH research queue.

## Candidates where guidance is interesting but 100X odds are poor at current starting size / valuation

Ather Energy, Milky Mist, Shadowfax, Sona BLW, Sansera, KRN Heat Exchanger, Sudeep Pharma (valuation caveat), Neuland, Quality Power, and many of the listed power/auto names fall into this bucket unless future economics materially exceed current expectations.

Examples:

- **Ather:** FY26 volumes 2.63 lakh units (+69%) and revenue ₹3,823 Cr (+66%), with Q4 EBITDA margin improving to -2.5%; however current market cap is ~₹63,000 Cr while the company was still loss-making in Q1 FY27. Great business optionality, poor 100X starting mathematics.
- **Milky Mist:** FY26/IPO story and Q1 FY27 growth are strong; Q1 revenue was ~₹973 Cr (+44%) and PAT ~₹65 Cr, but market cap is already ~₹19,400 Cr and P/E ~106x.
- **Shadowfax:** market cap ~₹15,200 Cr, P/E ~90x and ROCE ~10%; growth is good, but the return-on-capital profile is not yet compelling enough for 100X.
- **KRN Heat Exchanger:** market cap ~₹9,700 Cr and P/E ~100x; excellent growth runway may exist, but valuation leaves little room for execution error.

## Large-cap / effectively non-100X from today's base

ABB India, Siemens India, Siemens Energy India, Hitachi Energy India, Schneider Electric, Cummins India, CG Power, Bosch, Bajaj Auto, TVS Motor, Titan, Eternal and similar companies may be excellent businesses, but their current market caps make 50X/100X outcomes extremely demanding. They should not consume disproportionate 100X research time unless an extraordinary structural mispricing is identified.

## Low-priority / require much stronger evidence before inclusion

HOAC Foods, Sakar Healthcare, OBSC Perfection, Beta Drugs, Kwality Pharma, Aarti Pharma, Aditya Infotech, Sky Gold, Divgi TorqTransfer, Steel Strips, Gabriel India and similar names need a stronger company-specific moat / TAM / ROIC argument before being elevated. Small market cap alone is not enough.

**Omnitech Engineering** is newly listed and therefore deserves a separate post-IPO validation exercise rather than immediate inclusion; NSE confirms main-board listing in March 2026.

## Sector-bias check

This list is heavily biased toward Indian industrials, auto components, power equipment, electronics manufacturing and pharma. That is useful for discovery but risks duplicating existing exposure. The strongest candidates should therefore displace weaker names rather than simply increase the count of similar companies.

The most important underrepresented area from this list is **AI/cloud infrastructure** (E2E), while industrial/power/auto candidates are already plentiful.

## Verdict

**Best immediate deep dives:** E2E Networks, Shivalik Bimetal, Pitti Engineering, Yash Highvoltage, KSH International, Sudeep Pharma.

**Good businesses but valuation/size constrained:** Netweb, TD Power, Milky Mist, Ather, KRN, Shadowfax, Sona BLW, Sansera, HFCL, Sterlite Tech, Cupid.

**Do not add simply because of guidance:** the large-cap industrial/auto/consumer names.

## Key research principle reinforced

**Guidance -> opportunity hypothesis -> required economics -> probability of success -> valuation -> position decision.**

A management revenue target is not a 100X thesis until we can translate it into future market cap, revenue, margin, profit, reinvestment and realistic market share.
