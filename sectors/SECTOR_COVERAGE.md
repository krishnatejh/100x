## Rules

1. Do not add a company solely to improve diversification.
2. When a sector becomes heavily represented, require stronger company-level evidence before adding another candidate.
3. A fashionable sector does not receive a higher probability of success merely because its TAM is large.
4. Maintain a separate Frontier bucket for extreme technology/business uncertainty.
5. Run periodic blind hunts with no predefined sector or country and compare results against this matrix.
6. Global discovery should include India, USA and eventually other markets; India/USA are only the current repository scope.

## Current bias check

The initial universe is too small to draw meaningful concentration conclusions. MTAR creates early concentration around strategic engineering / energy / aerospace themes. This is a reason to actively search unrelated sectors before expanding the same thematic cluster, not a reason to reject MTAR.
