# 100X Investment Research

## What is 100X?

100X is a long-term, sector-neutral investment research project focused on identifying listed companies with credible potential for exceptional shareholder returns over 10–25+ years.

The goal is not to predict the next multibagger. The goal is to systematically search for a small number of exceptional businesses where opportunity, economics, execution potential and starting valuation may create asymmetric long-term returns.

## The Core Question

**What would have to be true for a relatively small company today to become 50–100X larger—and how realistic is that path?**

We start with business outcomes, not stock-price targets. We reverse-engineer the scale, revenue, profitability, market share and growth required for an exceptional outcome, then test whether those assumptions are realistic.

## How Companies Are Evaluated

- **Business Potential** — TAM, growth runway, scalability and long-term economics.
- **Probability of Success** — moat, management, execution, competitive position and financial strength.
- **Investment Attractiveness** — whether today's valuation leaves sufficient room for exceptional returns.
- **100X Mathematics** — the future business required to support a 50–100X outcome.
- **Failure Analysis** — actively looking for reasons the thesis may fail and evidence that contradicts it.

## Classifications

- **CORE** — High-conviction candidate with a credible path to exceptional long-term returns.
- **WATCH** — Interesting opportunity that needs stronger evidence, better valuation support or further execution proof.
- **FRONTIER** — Very high potential, but extremely high uncertainty.
- **REJECTED** — Investigated and found not to meet the framework; the reason is retained for future reference.

## Beyond the 100X Framework

The primary framework remains focused on plausible 50–100X+ outcomes. Occasionally, research may uncover a compelling investment case even when the strict 100X path is insufficient, uncertain or unproven.

**Beyond 100X is a discovery lens, not another classification or recommendation.** A company is surfaced only when a specific alternative investment case has been explicitly supported by research.

## Principles

- Long-term horizon: **10–25+ years**
- Global and sector-neutral search
- Preference for smaller companies where exceptional scale-up is mathematically more feasible
- Business quality matters more than fashionable narratives
- Primary-source-led research where possible
- Clear separation of **fact, inference and speculation**
- No confirmation bias: actively seek disconfirming evidence
- The universe is not fixed; stronger new candidates can replace existing ones

## Important

This project is for investment research and idea generation. It is **not investment advice, a stock tip service or a prediction system**. Exceptional outcomes are rare, uncertain and involve substantial risk.
